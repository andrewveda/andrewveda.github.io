Philosophy of Practice

The classroom is a design problem.

Every session is an intervention — designed to shift how a student thinks, not just what they know. Students learn best when they feel safe to explore, question, experiment, and improve.

My teaching blends clarity, curiosity, creativity, and agency. My current obsession: the cryptic crossword as a pedagogy of ambiguity — training students to hold multiple meanings simultaneously, to delay closure, to enjoy not knowing yet.
